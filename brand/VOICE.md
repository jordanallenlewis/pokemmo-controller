# Voice

Written for PokeMMO players, not engineers. Plain words, short sentences, the
thing they can do next.

- Say what happened and what to press. "Binds applied. Start PokeMMO." Not "Operation completed successfully."
- Game words where the game has them: Focus Ring, hotbar, wheel, Pokedex, Global Trade Link. Controller words as printed on the pad the player holds (Cross, not "button 0").
- Never claim more than was tested. "Seen by this browser" is not "works in game".
- Say it is unofficial, once, in the footer and in the About text: "PokeMMO Controller is an unofficial, fan-made tool, made by and for PokeMMO players. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the PokeMMO team."
- One input does one action. Say it as a promise, not a warning: "Each button does one thing."
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "simply", no "just".
- Errors: what failed, what is safe (nothing was changed), what to do.
